# Nero AE Splash Master

> Custom splash screen designer for Adobe After Effects 2021–2026

A lightweight Windows utility that lets you personalize the Adobe After Effects startup splash screen with built-in Nero artwork presets or your own custom image.

## Overview

**Nero AE Splash Master** is made for creators who want to give Adobe After Effects a custom look without editing system files manually. The app supports:

- Adobe After Effects 2021 to 2026
- Built-in Nero artwork presets
- Custom image upload support
- One-click apply and restore actions
- Simple Windows interface

## Features

- Choose target Adobe After Effects version
- Select from built-in Nero artwork presets
- Drop or browse a custom PNG, JPG, or WebP image
- Apply custom splash screen instantly
- Restore the original default splash screen anytime
- Clean, easy-to-use desktop interface

## Supported Versions

- Adobe After Effects 2021
- Adobe After Effects 2022
- Adobe After Effects 2023
- Adobe After Effects 2024
- Adobe After Effects 2025
- Adobe After Effects 2026

## How It Works

1. Launch the app
2. Select the Adobe After Effects version you want to modify
3. Choose a built-in Nero artwork preset or drop your own image
4. Click **Apply Custom Splash**
5. Restart Adobe After Effects to see the new splash screen

To revert:
- Click **Restore Default**
- Restart Adobe After Effects

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11
- Adobe After Effects installed
- PNG, JPG, or WebP image file for custom splash use
