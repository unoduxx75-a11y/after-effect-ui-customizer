# 🎬 NeroSplashMaster.exe

> **Customize Adobe After Effects UI splash screen with your own images** | After Effects 2021-2026 | Custom UI Branding | Image Loader

---

## ✨ What Is It?

**NeroSplashMaster.exe** is a powerful Windows application that lets you customize Adobe After Effects' opening splash screen and UI with your own custom images. Load any image you want, and it automatically updates After Effects' UI branding.

**Compatible with:** After Effects 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026

---

## 🎯 Features

- ✅ **Custom Splash Screen** - Replace After Effects opening splash with your image
- ✅ **User Image Loader** - Simple interface to choose and load custom images
- ✅ **Multi-Version Support** - Works on After Effects 2021-2026
- ✅ **Easy to Use** - No coding required
- ✅ **Fast & Lightweight** - Minimal system resources
- ✅ **Instant Preview** - See changes immediately

## 🚀 How to Use

### 1. Download & Run
```
Download NeroSplashMaster.exe
Double-click to launch
```

### 2. Select Your Image
- Click "Browse" or "Choose Image"
- Pick any PNG, JPG, or BMP file from your computer
- Preview appears on screen

### 3. Select After Effects Version
- Choose which After Effects version to customize (2021-2026)
- App detects installed versions automatically

### 4. Apply Changes
- Click "Apply" or "Update"
- After Effects splash screen is now customized
- Restart After Effects to see the change

---

## 📁 Image Requirements

- **Format:** PNG, JPG, BMP
- **Recommended Size:** 1920x1080 or 2560x1440
- **File Size:** Under 10MB
- **Color Mode:** RGB or RGBA (with transparency)

---

## 🎨 How It Works

1. You select an image from your computer
2. NeroSplashMaster.exe locates After Effects installation folders
3. Backs up original splash screen image
4. Replaces it with your custom image
5. Next time you open After Effects, your custom image appears

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **After Effects not detected** | Make sure After Effects is installed, try running as Administrator |
| **Changes not showing** | Restart After Effects completely |
| **Image looks stretched** | Resize image to match splash screen dimensions |
| **Need to revert** | App creates backup of original image |
